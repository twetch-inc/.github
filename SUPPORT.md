# Support

## Using Twetch

These repositories are for the code. For anything about the app itself — your
account, posts, the wallet, the market — reach us on X at
[**@twetchapp**](https://x.com/twetchapp).

The app lives at [twetch.com](https://twetch.com).

## Building on Twetch

If you're working with one of our SDKs, open an issue on that repository.
Include the version, what you called, what you expected, and what you got.

Questions about what's in scope are answered in that repo's `SCOPE.md` — it's
the contract, and it explains the deliberate omissions rather than leaving you
to guess.

## Security

Don't use either of the above for security issues. See
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) — use GitHub's private vulnerability reporting on the
repository's **Security** tab.

## Lost your seed phrase?

We can't help, and that's the design. Your seed is generated in your browser and
never reaches our servers, so there's no copy here to give back to you. Nobody at
Twetch will ever ask you for it — anyone who does is trying to rob you.
